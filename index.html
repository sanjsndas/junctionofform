    Design & Architecture FAQ

    How do I start planning a renovation?
    Begin with a detailed brief: how you live, what frustrates you, and what you refuse to compromise. Measure everything. Then prioritise structure, light, and flow before finishes. A good designer will spend more time on questions than answers in the first meeting.
    What is the most overlooked aspect of interior design?
    Lighting—specifically the quality and direction of both natural and artificial light. Most people choose fixtures before understanding how light will behave in a room. We recommend planning light as an architectural element, not an afterthought.
    Are sustainable materials worth the cost?
    Often, yes—especially when you factor in health and longevity. Breathable paints, solid timber, and natural fibres often outlast their cheaper counterparts. The upfront premium is frequently offset by lower replacement and healthcare costs.
    How do I make a small room feel larger?
    Prioritise sightlines. Keep furniture low, use consistent flooring, and let light travel. Mirrors help, but nothing beats removing unnecessary partitions. Sometimes the best move is to eliminate a door entirely.
