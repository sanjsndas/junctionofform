    • About Junction Of Forms

    An independent journal for architects, designers, and thoughtful homeowners—exploring the spaces we inhabit and the ideas that shape them.

    Junction Of Forms was founded in 2021 by architect Elena Marchetti and writer James Okonkwo, out of a shared frustration with the way design is typically discussed. Too much of the conversation was either superficial—focused on trends, shopping, and quick fixes—or impenetrable, written for a narrow academic audience. We wanted to create something different: a publication that takes design seriously, but speaks to anyone who cares about how spaces feel.

    The name comes from a simple observation: architecture and interior design are not separate disciplines. They are a single continuum. The way a building meets the sky, the way a door meets the floor, the way a chair meets the wall—these junctions are where design happens. They are the moments that determine whether a space feels calm or chaotic, generous or mean, alive or inert.

    “The best spaces are not the ones you notice. They are the ones that make you feel more like yourself.”

    What we publish

    We publish long-form essays, field guides, and case studies on a wide range of topics: spatial planning, materials, light, acoustics, furniture, sustainability, and the principles of beautiful, functional space. We are not interested in fads. We are interested in ideas that endure.

    Our articles are written for people who want to understand not just what works, but why. We believe that good design is not a matter of taste—it is a matter of attention. When you pay close attention to how people actually live, the right solutions become clear.

    Our editorial independence is non-negotiable. We do not accept sponsored content, we do not run advertisements, and we do not allow commercial interests to influence what we write. Our only obligation is to our readers.

    Our principles

    • Clarity over cleverness. We write in plain language, without jargon or pretension.
    • Depth over speed. We take the time to research, reflect, and revise. Each article is a considered piece, not a hot take.
    • Substance over style. We care about how things look, but we care more about how they work—and how they make people feel.
    • Independence over influence. We are reader-supported and beholden to no one.

    Who we are

    Junction Of Forms is a small, independent team. We are architects, designers, writers, and researchers. We have built, renovated, taught, and written about buildings for decades. We live in London and Milan, but our work takes us everywhere.

    Our commitment to you

    We are not here to sell you anything. We are here to help you see your own spaces more clearly—and to give you the tools to make them better. Whether you are planning a renovation, building a new home, or simply trying to make a single room feel right, we hope our work is useful to you.

    Thank you for reading.
