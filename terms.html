    Junction Of Forms is an independent editorial publication about interior design, architecture, and the art of living well. We are based in London and Milan. References to "we," "us," or "our" in these Terms refer to Junction Of Forms.

    We collect only the information necessary to operate the Site and deliver our content. This includes:

    We use minimal cookies to improve your experience on the Site. For full details, please see our Cookies Policy.

    We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

    • To send you our newsletter, if you have subscribed.
    • To respond to your inquiries and communications.
    • To analyse Site usage and improve our content and user experience.
    • To comply with legal obligations and protect our rights.

    We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. We do not use your information for advertising purposes.

    4. Legal basis for processing

    If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA) or the United Kingdom, our legal basis for processing your personal data is:

    • Consent: For newsletter subscriptions and any other processing where you have given clear consent.
    • Legitimate interests: For analysing Site usage and improving our services, provided your rights are not overridden.
    • Legal obligation: Where we are required to process data to comply with the law.

    5. Data retention

    We retain your personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes for which it was collected. Newsletter subscriber data is retained until you unsubscribe. Contact inquiries are retained for up to 12 months. Anonymous analytics data may be retained indefinitely.

    Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal information:

    • Right to access: You can request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
    • Right to rectification: You can request that we correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
    • Right to erasure: You can request that we delete your personal data.
    • Right to restrict processing: You can request that we limit how we use your data.
    • Right to data portability: You can request that we transfer your data to another organisation.
    • Right to object: You can object to our processing of your data based on legitimate interests.

    To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at privacy@junctionofforms.com. We will respond within 30 days.

    7. Third-party services

    We use a minimal number of third-party services to operate the Site:

    • Newsletter delivery: We use a third-party email service provider to deliver our newsletter. Your email address is shared with this provider solely for this purpose.
    • Analytics: We may use privacy-focused analytics tools that do not track individual users or share data with third parties.

    We do not use social media pixels, advertising trackers, or any other invasive tracking technologies.

    Your information may be transferred to and processed in countries outside your country of residence, including the United States, where our service providers may be located.
