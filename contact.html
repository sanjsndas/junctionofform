    Before you write
    How quickly do you respond?
    We aim to reply within two working days. If you’re pitching a time-sensitive story, please mention that in the subject line.
    Do you accept guest articles?
    Occasionally. We prefer pitches that are original, well-researched, and aligned with our editorial focus on spatial design, materials, and the experience of living. Send a short outline first.
    Can I republish your content?
    You may quote up to 100 words with a link back to the original article. For full republishing, please get in touch to discuss licensing.
