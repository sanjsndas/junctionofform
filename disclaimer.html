    • Cookies Policy

    Last updated: 15 March 2025  ·  Effective: 15 March 2025

    This Cookies Policy explains how Junction Of Forms (“we”, “us”, “our”) uses cookies and similar technologies on www.junctionofforms.com. It should be read alongside our Privacy Policy.

    We keep our use of cookies light and purposeful. We only use them to make the site work properly, understand which articles our readers enjoy, and remember your preferences. We don’t use them to track you across other websites or to serve you targeted advertising.

    In short: Essential cookies keep the site running. Analytics cookies help us understand what you read — and they’re anonymised. You can change or withdraw your consent at any time via your browser settings or by contacting us.

    1. What are cookies?

    Cookies are small text files that are stored on your device (computer, phone, or tablet) when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently, as well as to provide information to the site owners.

    Similar technologies include web beacons (tiny transparent images) and local storage, which can also store small amounts of data. In this policy, we use “cookies” to cover all these technologies.

    2. How we use cookies

    We use cookies for three main purposes:

    • Essential functionality — to keep the site secure and running smoothly.
    • Analytics — to understand how readers navigate the site, which articles are most popular, and where we can improve.
    • Preferences — to remember choices you make, such as dismissing a notice or setting a reading preference.

    3. Types of cookies we use

    Below is a detailed breakdown of the cookies we set and why.

    Cookie name Type Purpose Duration
    jof_session Essential Maintains your session while you browse the site, so pages load correctly. Session
    jof_cookie_consent Essential Stores your cookie preferences so we don’t ask you every time you visit. 12 months
    jof_analytics Analytics Anonymised tracking of page views, time on page, and referral source. No personal identifiers. 26 months
    jof_prefs Preference Remembers small UI choices, like whether you’ve dismissed a notice. 6 months

    We do not use advertising or targeting cookies. If we ever introduce them, we will update this policy and ask for your explicit consent first.

    4. Third-party cookies

    We keep third-party cookies to an absolute minimum. Currently, we use:

    • Analytics provider — a privacy-focused analytics service that sets anonymised cookies to help us measure readership. This data cannot be used to identify you personally.
    • Embedded content — if we embed a video or social media post, those platforms may set their own cookies. We have no control over these; please check the relevant platform’s cookie policy.

    5. Managing and disabling cookies

    You have the right to decide whether to accept or reject cookies. You can manage your preferences in several ways:

    • Browser settings — most browsers allow you to block or delete cookies. Look for the “Privacy” or “Security” settings in your browser menu. Note that blocking essential cookies may affect how the site works.
    • Opt-out of analytics — you can use browser add-ons or settings to prevent analytics cookies from being set. Our analytics provider also offers a direct opt-out.
    • Withdraw consent — if you previously consented to non-essential cookies, you can change your mind at any time by clearing cookies in your browser and revisiting our site, or by contacting us directly.

    For more detailed guidance, visit aboutcookies.org or allaboutcookies.org.

    6. What happens if you disable cookies?

    If you disable or block cookies, some parts of the site may not function as intended. For example:

    • Essential cookies are required for the site to load correctly — without them, you may not be able to browse or use certain features.
    • Analytics cookies help us improve the site. If you block them, your visit won’t be counted, but the site will still work.
    • Preference cookies remember your settings. If you block them, you may need to re-enter preferences on each visit.

    7. Updates to this policy

    We may update this Cookies Policy from time to time to reflect changes in technology, regulation, or our practices. The “Last updated” date at the top of this page will always reflect the most recent version. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
