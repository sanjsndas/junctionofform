    • Privacy Policy

    Last updated: 15 March 2025  ·  Effective: 15 March 2025

    Junction Of Forms (“we”, “us”, “our”) respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This policy explains what we collect, why we collect it, and how you can exercise your rights.

    We keep things simple: we only ask for information we genuinely need, we never sell your data, and we aim to be transparent about every tool and process we use. This policy applies to www.junctionofforms.com and any related services that link to it.

    In short: We collect minimal analytics, we use cookies to improve your reading experience, and we only store your email if you choose to subscribe or contact us. You can request deletion at any time by writing to privacy@junctionofforms.com.

    1. Data we collect

    We collect information in three ways: information you give us, information collected automatically, and information from third parties.

    Information you give us

    • Contact form submissions — your name, email address, subject, and message content when you write to our editorial team.
    • Newsletter subscriptions — your email address and, optionally, your first name.
    • Comments and feedback — any information you voluntarily provide in response to an article.

    Information collected automatically

    • Analytics data — pages viewed, time on page, referring URL, approximate location (country/city level), device type, and browser. We use privacy-friendly analytics that do not track you across other websites.
    • Cookies and similar technologies — small files stored on your device. See our Cookies Policy for full details.

    Information from third parties

    We do not purchase or receive personal data from data brokers. If you interact with our social media accounts (Instagram, Pinterest, Substack), those platforms may share aggregated, anonymised insights with us.

    2. How we use your data

    We use your personal data only for the purposes listed below, and only when we have a lawful basis to do so under the UK GDPR and EU GDPR.

    Purpose Data used Lawful basis
    Responding to your inquiry Name, email, message Legitimate interest / consent
    Sending our newsletter Email, first name Consent (you opt in)
    Understanding what readers enjoy Anonymised analytics Legitimate interest
    Improving site performance & security Technical logs, cookies Legitimate interest
    Complying with legal obligations Any relevant records Legal obligation

    We will never use your data for automated decision-making or profiling that produces legal or similarly significant effects.

    3. Cookies & similar technologies

    We use a minimal set of cookies to keep the site running smoothly and to understand which articles resonate with readers. You can control cookies through your browser settings at any time.

    • Essential cookies — required for basic site functionality (e.g. remembering your cookie preferences). These cannot be switched off.
    • Analytics cookies — help us see which pages are read most, so we can plan future stories. These are anonymised and aggregated.
    • Preference cookies — remember choices you make, such as dismissing a notice.

    For a full breakdown, including how to withdraw consent, please read our Cookies Policy.

    4. Sharing your data

    We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. We share it only with trusted service providers who help us operate the site, and only to the extent necessary:

    • Email delivery provider — to send our newsletter (only if you subscribe).
    • Analytics provider — to collect anonymised usage statistics.
    • Hosting provider — to store the website and its data securely.

    All processors are bound by data-processing agreements and are required to protect your information. We may also disclose data if required by law or to protect our legal rights.

    5. International transfers

    Some of our service providers may store data outside the UK or European Economic Area (EEA). When that happens, we ensure appropriate safeguards are in place — such as UK adequacy regulations, the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, or standard contractual clauses — so your data remains protected.

    6. How long we keep your data

    • Contact form messages — kept for up to 24 months, then deleted, unless we are in an ongoing conversation.
    • Newsletter subscriptions — kept until you unsubscribe, plus a short period to honour your request.
    • Analytics data — retained in anonymised form for up to 26 months.
    • Legal records — kept as long as required by applicable law.

    7. Your rights

    Depending on where you live, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

    • Access — request a copy of the data we hold about you.
    • Rectification — ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
    • Erasure — ask us to delete your data (“right to be forgotten”).
    • Restriction — ask us to limit how we use your data.
    • Portability — receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
    • Objection — object to processing based on legitimate interests.
    • Withdraw consent — where processing is based on consent, you can withdraw it at any time.

    To exercise any of these rights, email privacy@junctionofforms.com. We will respond within 30 days. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority — in the UK, that’s the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO).

    8. Security

    We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your data — including HTTPS encryption, access controls, and regular reviews of our service providers. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, but we work hard to keep your information safe.

    9. Children’s privacy

    Our content is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

    10. Changes to this policy

    We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or the law. The “Last updated” date at the top of this page will always reflect the most recent version. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

    11. Contact us

    If you have any questions about this policy or how we handle your data, please get in touch:

    • Email: privacy@junctionofforms.com
    • Post: Junction Of Forms, 12 Clerkenwell Green, London EC1R 0DP, United Kingdom

    For general editorial inquiries, please use our contact page.
